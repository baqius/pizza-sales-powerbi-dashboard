# Pizza Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that turns a year of pizza sales data (January to December 2015) into clear answers about **when customers order, what they buy, and which products drive or drag revenue**.

---

## Business Problem

A pizza restaurant wants to understand its sales performance so it can plan staffing, stock and promotions. The dashboard answers four questions:

1. How much revenue and order volume does the business generate, and what does a typical order look like?
2. On which days and in which months is demand highest and lowest?
3. Which pizza categories and sizes contribute most to sales?
4. Which pizzas are the best and worst performers?

## Dashboard Overview

The report has two interactive pages, with filters for **Pizza Category** and **Order Date**.

| Page | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Overview** | Headline KPIs, daily and monthly order trends, sales share by category and by size, and total pizzas sold by category |
| **Best and Worst Sellers** | Top 5 and bottom 5 pizzas by revenue, quantity and number of orders, with written takeaways |

## Key Insights

**Timing of demand**
- **Friday is the busiest day** with about 3.5K orders, followed by Thursday and Saturday at about 3.2K each. **Sunday is the quietest** at about 2.6K.
- **July is the peak month** (1,935 orders), followed by May (1,853) and January (1,845). **October is the lowest** (1,646), with September close behind (1,661).

**What customers buy**
- **Classic is the top category**, with the highest share of sales (26.91%) and the most pizzas sold (14.89K). Supreme (25.46%), Chicken (23.96%) and Veggie (23.68%) are closely matched.
- **Large pizzas drive almost half of sales (45.89%)**, followed by Medium (30.49%) and Regular (21.77%). X-Large contributes only 1.72%.

**Best and worst performers**
- **The Thai Chicken Pizza earns the most revenue** (about 43K).
- **The Classic Deluxe Pizza sells the most units** (about 2.5K) and is also the most ordered.
- **The Brie Carre Pizza is the weakest performer** on revenue, quantity (490 units) and orders (480). Its volume is roughly half that of the next-lowest pizza.

## Recommendations

These are suggestions based on the patterns above.

1. **Staff and stock up for Thursday to Saturday**, especially Friday, when demand peaks.
2. **Run promotions on Sunday and Monday** to lift the quieter days, and in **September and October** when monthly orders dip.
3. **Lead with Large and Classic in menus and combos**, since they drive the largest share of sales.
4. **Review the Brie Carre Pizza**: reposition, promote or replace it, as it sells far below every other item.
5. **Protect and promote the Thai Chicken Pizza and Classic Deluxe Pizza**, the top earner and top seller.
